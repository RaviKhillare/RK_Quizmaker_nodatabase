IPv4 vs IPv6 Address

What is the difference in bit length and notation format?

Tap card to reveal answer
Networking Answer & Key Takeaways
An IP address uniquely identifies each device on a network.
  • IPv4 uses 32 bits divided into 4 decimal octets.
  • IPv6 uses 128 bits divided into 8 hexadecimal groups.
Example: 192.168.1.1 (IPv4) vs 2001:0db8::8a2e (IPv6)
